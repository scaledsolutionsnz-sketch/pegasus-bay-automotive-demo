What we do

Everything your vehicle needs, under one roof

One workshop for the lot, so you're not bouncing between places to keep your car on the road and road-legal.

01

WOF Inspections

Warrant of Fitness checks while you wait, with a clear rundown of any work needed to pass, no pressure to do it all at once.

02

Servicing & Tune-ups

Logbook and general servicing for petrol and diesel, oil, filters, fluids and a full once-over to catch small things before they cost you.

03

Brakes & Suspension

Pads, discs, shocks and steering sorted so the car stops straight and rides the way it should on Canterbury roads.

04

Engine Diagnostics

Warning light on? We plug in, read the fault properly and tell you what it actually means before quoting any repair.

05

Batteries & Electrical

Flat batteries, alternators, starters and wiring gremlins tested and replaced, including cold-morning no-starts.

06

Cambelts & Clutches

Cambelt replacements done on time to protect your engine, plus clutch repairs and replacements so the car pulls away and changes gear smoothly.

About the workshop

Your local Kaiapoi mechanic, straight up and easy to deal with

Pegasus Bay Automotive is a Kaiapoi workshop looking after everyday vehicles for North Canterbury drivers. Servicing, WOFs, repairs and diagnostics, the work that keeps your car safe, reliable and out of the garage.

We keep it simple: we tell you what your car needs in plain language, what it can wait on, and what it'll cost before we touch a spanner.

  • Locally owned and operated in Kaiapoi
  • Clear quotes before any work starts
  • All makes and models, petrol and diesel
