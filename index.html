About the workshop

Your local Kaiapoi mechanic — straight up and easy to deal with

Pegasus Bay Automotive is a Kaiapoi workshop looking after everyday vehicles for North Canterbury drivers. Servicing, WOFs, repairs and diagnostics — the work that keeps your car safe, reliable and out of the garage.

We keep it simple: we tell you what your car needs in plain language, what it can wait on, and what it'll cost before we touch a spanner.
